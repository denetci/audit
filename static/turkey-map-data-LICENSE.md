`turkey-map-data.js` is the derived boundary dataset, distributed under the same
ODbL 1.0 license. Original longitude/latitude rings are projected using Mercator
and scaled to a 1000-unit SVG viewBox; coordinates are rounded to two decimals.
Display names Afyon and Hakkari are normalized to Afyonkarahisar and Hakkâri.
No administrative boundaries have been invented. This visualization is not an
authoritative administrative boundary source.

Audit and monitoring records are separate application data and are not included
in this boundary dataset.
